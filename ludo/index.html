House rules

  • 4 players (red, green, yellow, blue), 4 tokens each. Race all four round the board and up your home column first.
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out onto your start square. A 6 also gives you another roll, but three 6s in a row lose your turn.
  • Land on another player's token and it goes back to their base, except on start squares and ★ star squares, which are safe.
  • You need the exact roll to reach the finish in the middle.
  • Points: every other player pays the winner 1 point for each token they haven't brought home.
  • House rules you can switch on: a 1 also brings a token out, a capture earns another roll, two tokens make a block, you must capture when you can, bringing a token home earns another roll, two of your tokens on one square are safe.
