Board games

Ludo, Checkers and Gaple to out-think each other; Werewolf, Undercover and Heist for the whole group. Back to the card tables.

Ludo

Roll a six, race your four tokens round the board and home before anyone else. Land on a rival to send them back.

  • 4 players: friends, with bots in empty seats
  • Classic rules, with house options you can switch on
  • Played for pretend dollars, settled up after

Gaple

Indonesian dominoes for four. Match the ends of the line, block your rivals, and make them pay for every pip they are left holding.

  • 4 players: friends, with bots in empty seats
  • Go out on a tile that fits both ends for double pay
  • Played for pretend dollars, settled up after
Play vs bots Play with friends

Werewolf

Secret roles for 5 to 12 friends. The wolves hunt by night, the village votes by day. The app runs it all, so nobody has to moderate.

  • Werewolves, Seer, Doctor and Villagers
  • Play together or remotely, with a room chat
  • Optional bots, a graveyard chat for the dead

Undercover

Everyone gets a secret word, but one of you has a slightly different one. Give clues, spot the odd one out, vote them out.

  • 4 to 12 friends, at a table or on a call
  • Words in Bahasa Indonesia or English: everyone picks
  • Optional Mr. White, and your own word pairs

Checkers

The classic diagonal duel: capture is compulsory, crown your pieces at the far row. Dam rules with flying kings, or classic English.

  • Bots from Easy to Hard
  • Two players on one device, or online
  • The host picks Dam or English rules

Heist

A crew pulls five heists, but undercover cops are among them. Pick teams, vote, and work out who keeps sabotaging the job. Nobody gets knocked out.

  • 5 to 10 friends, at a table or on a call
  • Optional Mastermind and Sniper roles
  • Optional bots, a private chat for the cops
