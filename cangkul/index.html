How to play

  • 2 to 4 players, one deck. Everyone gets 7 cards; the rest is the face-down stock. Get rid of your cards.
  • Whoever leads plays any card. Going round, everyone else must play a card of the same suit.
  • No card of that suit? You cangkul ("dig"): draw from the stock one card at a time, keeping every card, until you turn up the suit. That card is played right away.
  • You can't dig while you hold a card of the suit.
  • The highest card wins the trick (aces are high, 2s are low). The winner leads the next one.
  • Stock empty and you still can't follow? Either you pick up every card played in the trick so far and miss your turn, or you just skip: pick which when you deal. If no card is left on the table, the leader leads again.
  • Play your last card and you're out, and safe.
  • First one out wins: the game stops at the end of that trick, and everyone else pays the winner 1 point per card still in hand.
  • Last one holding loses (pick it when you deal): play goes on until one player is left with cards. Places pay: with 4 players 1st +3, 2nd +1, 3rd −1, last −3; with 3 players +2, 0, −2; with 2 players +1, −1.
  • The first game is led by a random player; after that, the last winner leads.
