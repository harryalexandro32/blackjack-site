House rules

  • Hong Kong rules with 144 tiles: three suits (dots, bamboo, characters), winds, dragons, and 4 flowers + 4 seasons.
  • A winning hand is 4 sets and a pair. Sets: a chow (three in a row, one suit), a pung (three the same) or a kong (four the same). Seven pairs and thirteen orphans also win.
  • On your turn you draw, then discard. Anyone can claim a discard to win, or for a pung or kong; only the next player can claim it for a chow. A win beats a pung, a pung beats a chow.
  • Flowers and seasons are set aside when drawn and replaced. Kongs draw a replacement tile.
  • Faan: self-drawn 1, concealed 1, no flowers 1, own flower or season 1 each, a full set of flowers or seasons 2, dragon pung 1, seat or round wind pung 1, all chows 1, last tile 1, kong replacement 1, robbing a kong 1, all pungs 3, mixed one suit 3, seven pairs 4, small three dragons 5, pure one suit 7, big three dragons 8. Limit hands are 10.
  • Payment: 0 faan 1 point, 1 → 2, 2 → 4, 3 → 8, 4 → 16, 5 → 24, 6 → 32, 7 → 48, 8 → 64, 9 → 96, 10 → 128. Self-drawn: the other three each pay. Off a discard: the discarder pays double.
  • The dealer keeps the deal after a win or a drawn hand; the round wind moves after everyone has dealt.
