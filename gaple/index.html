House rules

  • 4 players, 7 tiles each from a double-six set of 28. There's nothing to draw from.
  • The first game starts with whoever holds the double six (or the double blank, if you pick that): they have to play it. After that, the last winner starts with any tile.
  • On your turn, play a tile that matches one of the two open ends of the line. When a tile fits both ends, you choose the end.
  • If nothing you hold fits, you have to pass. You can't pass while you hold a tile that fits.
  • Play your last tile to win. If nobody can play, the game is blocked: the fewest pips left wins. A tie goes to fewer tiles, then to the tied player who comes first after the last one to play.
  • Points: every loser pays the winner 1 point per pip left in hand.
  • Lone double blank (an option): if the double blank is the only tile you're left with, it counts 25 instead of 0.
  • Gaple (an option): go out with a tile that fits both open ends and everyone pays double.
