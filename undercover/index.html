How it plays

  • Most players are Civilians and share one word, like Kopi. The Undercover gets a close but different word, like Teh, and isn't told they're the odd one out. (1 undercover with 4–6 players, 2 with 7–9, 3 with 10–12.)
  • Mr. White (the host can switch this off) gets no word at all and has to bluff. Mr. White never gives the first clue.
  • Each round, everyone gives one clue in turn: typed, or said out loud at the table. Don't say your word, and don't make it too easy.
  • Talk it over, then vote. The most votes is out and their role is shown. A tie goes to a runoff between the tied players; a second tie means nobody.
  • Mr. White, when voted out, gets one guess at the civilians' word: right, and Mr. White wins.
  • Civilians win when every undercover and Mr. White is out. The infiltrators win when the civilians are down to as few as them.
  • Points: a civilian win is worth 2, Mr. White 6, the undercover 10.
  • Words come in Bahasa Indonesia and English: everyone picks which they see.
