How it plays

  • 8×8 board, dark squares only, 12 pieces each. Light moves first. Pieces move one square diagonally forward.
  • Capturing is compulsory. Jump an enemy piece to an empty square behind it, and keep jumping while you can. If there are several ways to capture, pick any.
  • Reach the far row and your piece becomes a king, which moves backwards too.
  • Dam (the usual Indonesian way): kings fly any distance along a diagonal and capture from afar, landing anywhere past the piece. Men capture backwards as well as forwards.
  • Classic English: kings move one square at a time; men only capture forwards. Being crowned ends your move.
  • You lose when you have no pieces or can't move. 25 moves each with only kings moving and no capture is a draw.
