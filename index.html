By the book–

No money here. Net units count a win as +1, a double as ±2, a blackjack as +1.5 and a surrender as −0.5, so you can see how a strategy holds up. "By the book" is how often your play matched basic strategy.

How the table plays

  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Aces count 1 or 11, face cards 10.
  • With an ace or ten showing, the dealer peeks for blackjack before you act.
  • Double on any first two cards: one more card, then you stand.
  • Split any pair, including mixed tens, up to four hands. Split aces get one card each and can't be resplit; ace + ten after a split is 21, not blackjack.
  • Surrender gives up half a hand, only as your first decision.
  • The shoe reshuffles when the cut card comes out, about 75% of the way through.
