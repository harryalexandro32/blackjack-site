Capsa Banting

Be the first to get rid of all 13 cards. The 2 is king, and bombs beat everything, even a single 2.

  • 4 players: friends, with bots in empty seats
  • House rules: 3♦ starts, bombs, no finishing on a single 2
  • Points per card left, settled in pretend dollars

Cangkul

Follow the suit that was led. No card of that suit? Dig through the deck until you find one. First to empty their hand wins.

  • 2 to 4 players: bots or friends
  • Winner mode, or last one holding cards loses
  • Points per card left, settled in pretend dollars

Mahjong

Hong Kong rules with flowers: build four sets and a pair, claim the right discards, and score faan.

  • 4 players: friends, with bots in empty seats
  • Chow, pung, kong, flowers and seasons
  • Win with any hand, or play to a 3-faan minimum
