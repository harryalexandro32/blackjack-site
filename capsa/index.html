House rules

  • 4 players, 13 cards each. Get rid of all your cards first to win.
  • Card order: 3 lowest … K, A, 2 highest. Suits: ♠ > ♥ > ♣ > ♦.
  • The first game starts with whoever holds the 3♦, and the first play must include it. After that, the last winner starts.
  • Play the same kind as the table: single on single, pair on pair, three on three, 5-card package on package. Once you pass, you're out until the round ends.
  • Packages, low to high: straight, flush, full house, bomb four of a kind (4 cards), bomb straight flush.
  • Straights: 2-3-4-5-6 is the lowest, J-Q-K-A-2 the highest. A-2-3-4-5 doesn't count.
  • A bomb beats any package or a single 2. Bombing a single 2 ends the game: the bombed player loses.
  • You can't finish on a single 2.
  • Double pairs (pick it when you deal): two pairs of neighbouring ranks, like 3-3-4-4 or 10-10-J-J (3-3-J-J doesn't count). With a spare card it's a 5-card package, the weakest one, below a straight. With no spare it's just the 4 cards, and only a higher double pair beats it. The higher pair's top card decides.
  • Points: losers pay 1 per card left (each 2 counts 2), ×2 with 10+ cards, ×3 with all 13. Bombed on a 2: that player pays the bomber double.
  • Loser mode (pick it when you deal): going out doesn't end the game. Play goes on until one player is left holding cards. Places pay: 1st +3, 2nd +1, 3rd −1, last −3. A bomb on a single 2 still ends it: the bomber takes the best open place, the rest go by fewest cards, the bombed player is last.
