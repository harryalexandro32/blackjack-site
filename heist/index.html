How it plays

  • You're a crew of thieves pulling up to 5 heists. Some of you are secretly undercover cops (2 with 5–6 players, 3 with 7–9, 4 with 10), and the cops know each other.
  • Each heist, the leader picks a team of the size shown. Everyone votes in the open to approve it or not; a majority sends it.
  • On the heist, each team member secretly plays Success or Sabotage. The crew can only play Success; cops can play either. One Sabotage fails the heist (with 7+ players, the 4th heist needs two).
  • A team that isn't approved passes the lead on. Five rejected teams in a row and the cops win.
  • The crew wins with 3 successful heists, the cops with 3 failed ones. Nobody is knocked out: everyone plays to the end.
  • Special roles (a host option): the Mastermind is crew and knows who the cops are. The Sniper is a cop: if the crew wins 3 heists, the Sniper gets one shot at naming the Mastermind and stealing the win. So the Mastermind has to steer without being obvious.
